TERCOM is a 1970s vector CRT-inspired terrain watchface for Pebble Time 2. Its green wireframe landscape sits beneath a yellow flight-path reticle and a large digital clock.

Choose from five terrain tracks: Crags, Canyon, Dunes, Caldera, and Alpine. Crags and Alpine have finer wireframe detail. A quick double wrist tap cycles tracks and saves the selection. A single tap starts a brief forward-flight animation that returns to its resting view. The clock updates each minute while idle.

The terrain and pitch ladder respond to wrist tilt during flight mode. The time follows your 12/24-hour setting. The lower bar shows compass heading when available, daily steps, and battery percentage. The ALT figure is a stylized value derived from steps; it is not a measured altitude.

The animated preview cycles through the five terrain tracks. On the watch, tracks change only when you double tap. Compatible with Pebble Time 2 (Emery).
