TERCOM is an authentic 1970s phosphor vector CRT topographic guidance watchface for the Pebble Time 2 (Emery), inspired by early cruise missile terrain-contour matching and Evans & Sutherland visual flight simulation HUDs.

FEATURES:
- 5 Selectable Terrain Bundle Tracks (Double-tap wrist to cycle & save):
  * TRK-01 CRAGS: The beloved original dual craggy mountain peaks with center pass.
  * TRK-02 CANYON: Sheer gorge corridor with flat riverbed canyon floor.
  * TRK-03 DUNES: Sinuous transverse sand dunes with shifting ripple harmonics.
  * TRK-04 CALDERA: Sunken volcanic crater basin with elevated rim and central cone.
  * TRK-05 ALPINE: Sawtooth multi-summit alpine massif with jagged ridgelines.
- Dual-Telemetry 6-Line Altitude Ladder:
  * Left side: Continuous hour reference scale centered on the current hour.
  * Right side: 5-minute telemetry intervals (:25..:00 or :55..:30).
  * Top HUD director chevron and horizontal baseline ticks.
- Flight-Path Reticle & Vector Digital Clock: Bold yellow flight director reticle framing hours and minutes in clean digital typography. Supports 12-hour and 24-hour modes.
- Full Flight Complications:
  * Top bar: Active track telemetry ('TRK-01:CRAGS') and barometric cruise altitude ('ALT: 1,280FT').
  * Bottom bar: Live compass heading ('HDG: 284°'), daily step count ('STP: 9,210'), and battery percentage ('BAT: 86%').
- Gesture & Battery Optimization:
  * Single wrist flick: Wakes up 4.5s 25 FPS active forward flight animation with live horizon banking.
  * Quick double wrist flick: Cycles to next terrain track with haptic pulse and saves to flash.
  * Idle: 1-minute static redraws when still to maintain 7+ day battery life.
