Battery and settings fixes. A new Compass setting lets you switch the compass off, and a steady face no longer redraws for small heading jitter. Stray wrist taps no longer chain flights back to back, and one flick no longer counts as a double tap. Saving settings now keeps the track you picked on the watch unless you choose a new one. The Canyon course no longer jumps backward during flight. Pebble 2 hides the heading slot, since it has no compass.
